# Songnado

A music-trivia party game for iOS. Play a 30-second clip, and the first
team to name the song and the artist takes the round.

No account, no signup — pick your packs, name your teams, and play.

## How it works

Songnado runs on one phone that gets passed around. Choose from **14
packs and over 6,000 songs**: six decades of hits, movie soundtracks and
songs from movies, TV themes, Broadway, wedding and road-trip sets, and
All-Time Hits. Mix as many packs as you like.

**Four ways to play**

- **Classic** — take turns, score for the song, the artist, or both
- **Blitz** — the clock is the scoreboard; answer faster, score higher
- **Elimination** — draft your board, protect your strengths, race to
  clear your grid
- **Buzz** — every team on their own phone, first thumb on the buzzer
  gets the answer

Explicit tracks are filtered out, so it's safe to hand to anyone.

## About the music

Songnado plays 30-second preview clips streamed from Deezer's public
catalogue, so an internet connection is required. Songnado is an
independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by
Deezer or any other music service.
