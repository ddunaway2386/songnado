# Songnado

A music-trivia party game for iOS. A song plays from Apple Music, and the
first team to name the song and the artist takes the round.

No Songnado account, no signup — pick your packs, name your teams, and
play. An Apple Music subscription is required.

## How it works

Songnado runs on one phone that gets passed around. Choose from **15
packs and over 5,600 songs**: six decades of hits, movie soundtracks and
songs from movies, TV themes, Broadway, wedding and road-trip sets,
Christmas hits and All-Time Hits. Mix as many packs as you like.

**Three ways to play**

- **Classic** — take turns, score for the song, the artist, or both
- **Blitz** — the clock is the scoreboard; answer faster, score higher
- **Elimination** — draft your board, protect your strengths, race to
  clear your grid

Songs Apple Music marks as explicit are left out, and clean versions are
used where they exist.
